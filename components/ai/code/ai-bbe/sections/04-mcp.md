  Make your integrations available to any AI application. Publish business capabilities as Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools, and let your own agents use tools from other MCP servers.

  - **MCP servers from your services**: tool names, descriptions, and input schemas come from your code and its documentation.
  - **MCP clients for your agents**: connect Ballerina agents to external MCP servers and choose which tools they can use.
  - **Secured access**: protect tools with standard authentication and scopes.
